Calculate works out how many of the chosen projector the ticked parts need, places them, picks lenses and warps the picture.

Each block is a separate part of the model; type a size to change it. Open «Move and turn» to place a part somewhere else or turn it. Untick «Show» to take a part away. Untick «Project» to leave a part dark: it still stands there and casts shadows. «Split» cuts a part into the pieces that do not touch each other.

W2Projectors

Click a number to select a projector. Hold Ctrl and click to select several; they then move together, and model, portrait, fit and remove apply to all of them.

Placing cuts the model's outline into fields, one projector per field, and turns a projector to portrait when its field is taller than wide. Blend overlap is the share of each picture that is shared with its neighbour and faded out; changing it places the projectors again.

W6The site in 3D

Drag an arrow to move the selected projector along one axis (red X, green height, blue Z), or a small square to move in a plane. «Move part» and «Turn part» put the handles on the part whose name you clicked in box W1. «Tilt and pan» swaps the arrows for rings: drag a ring to tilt the projector up or down or pan it sideways. Click a projector's number to select it. Drag elsewhere to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. With the mouse over this box the arrow keys turn the camera around the model, Shift + arrows slide it sideways and up or down, and + and − zoom. The white marker is the audience.

      W11How Panasonic projectors warp

      • Geometric Adjustment is built into the projector and run from the remote: corner adjustment, keystone and curved correction for pillars, cylinders and spheres.
      • Free Grid is built into selected models. A grid of 2 × 2 up to 17 × 17 lines is laid over the picture and each node is moved until the picture sits right on the surface.
      • Geometry Manager Pro is free Windows software that does the same from a laptop over Ethernet. It is now part of Panasonic's Visual Software Suite.
      • The ET-UK20 upgrade kit raised the grid to as many as 41 × 26 lines and added masking. Panasonic states that ET-UK20 and ET-CUK10 are discontinued in the second quarter of 2026.
      • A grid bends a flat picture smoothly. Sharp steps in depth on a 3D object cannot be followed by any grid. For those the picture must be rendered from the projector's own position, as box W7 does, and played out by a media server.

      Test theme: a planning aid, not a guarantee. Throw data comes unchanged from the Throw Planner catalog; the light cone starts where the throw formula puts it. Projectors are aimed straight at the model's centre and may be tilted, since warping removes the keystone. Model support for Geometric Adjustment and Free Grid follows Panasonic's compatibility table dated May 2024; models missing from that table are shown as not listed. Brightness in the 3D view is relative, not measured. Nothing is uploaded: the model file stays in your browser.
